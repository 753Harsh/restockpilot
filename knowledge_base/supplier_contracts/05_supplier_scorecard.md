# How Suppliers Are Ranked

## Score
Supplier score = 40 percent price + 30 percent fill rate + 20 percent on-time rate + 10 percent lead time.
Lower price, higher fill rate, higher on-time rate and shorter lead time all raise the score.

## Status first
Only "approved" suppliers can be auto-selected. A "new" supplier can be suggested but always needs a manager. A "blocked" supplier can never be chosen.

## Risk from news
If market news says a supplier has a plant shutdown, strike or quality problem, move the order to the next best approved supplier for that category.
