# Fruits and Vegetables Supplier Profiles

## Azadpur Fresh Traders (SUP-03)
Approved. MSME. Buys from Azadpur mandi every night and delivers by 6 am (lead time 1 day). Payment 15 days. Fill rate 90 percent. Prices follow the mandi rate of the day. Usual supplier for tomato and banana, and the fast backup for onion and potato.

## Kisan Direct FPO (SUP-04)
Approved. A farmer producer organisation from Haryana. About 10 percent cheaper than mandi traders but lead time is 2 days and fill rate is 85 percent. Usual supplier for potato and onion, not for tomato or banana which spoil fast. Payment 7 days. When onion or potato stock is very low, use Azadpur Fresh Traders instead because they deliver next morning.

## NewLeaf Agro Ventures (SUP-11)
New supplier, not yet vetted. Claims MSME status. Offers 20 percent lower prices and 90 days credit. No delivery history. Needs vendor onboarding and a manager decision before any order.
