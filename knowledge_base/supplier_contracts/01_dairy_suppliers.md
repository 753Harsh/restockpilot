# Dairy Supplier Profiles

## Yamuna Dairy Co-op (SUP-01)
Approved since 2023. Registered MSME. Supplies toned milk, curd and eggs. Daily morning delivery (lead time 1 day). Payment 30 days. Fill rate 97 percent, on-time 95 percent. Has a full cold chain. Preferred supplier for milk and curd.

## Shree Ganesh Dairy (SUP-02)
Approved since 2024. Registered MSME. Supplies paneer and milk at about 3 percent lower price. Lead time 1 day, payment 30 days. Fill rate 92 percent. Backup supplier for milk. Main supplier for paneer. If its plant is shut, switch paneer to Yamuna Dairy Co-op.
