# Paying MSME Suppliers on Time

## The law
Under section 15 of the MSMED Act, 2006, a buyer must pay a registered micro or small supplier within the agreed period, and the agreed period cannot be more than 45 days from the day the goods are accepted. If no period is agreed, the limit is 15 days.

## Tax effect
From financial year 2023-24 (assessment year 2024-25), section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 allows a business to claim the expense for a payment to a micro or small enterprise only in the year it is actually paid, if it is paid late. Late payment also attracts compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate.

## ZipBasket rule
- Never accept payment terms above 45 days from an MSME supplier, even if the supplier offers them.
- A "90 days credit" offer from an MSME supplier is a warning sign, not a benefit.
- Record the MSME status of every supplier in the supplier master.
