# Stock Limits on Essential Commodities

## Background
Under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955, the central government can notify stock limits for traders and retailers of items like pulses, wheat and edible oil when prices rise. In 2024 a stock limit order applied to tur and chana dal, with a limit of 5 tonnes for each retail outlet.

## ZipBasket rule
- Pulses: never hold more than 5 tonnes per store (a dark store holds far less, but the check stays in place).
- If news reports a new stock limit order, treat it as a risk flag and send the order for manager approval.
- Do not hoard: do not order more than the reorder policy suggests just because prices are rising.
