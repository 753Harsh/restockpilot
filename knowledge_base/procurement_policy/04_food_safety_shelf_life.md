# Food Safety and Shelf Life at Receiving

## Licence
Every dark store holds an FSSAI licence and follows the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. Expired food must never be sold.

## Minimum remaining shelf life (company rule)
Perishable goods must arrive with at least 70 percent of their shelf life left. A store must not order more of a perishable SKU than it can sell within 70 percent of its shelf life.

## FEFO
Pick first-expiry-first-out. Stock that expires in the next 2 days is moved to the clearance list and is not counted as sellable stock.

## Cold chain
Dairy and frozen goods must come from suppliers with a cold chain. Frozen goods come only from frozen suppliers.
