# Pricing and Legal Metrology

## MRP
Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, packaged goods must carry the MRP and must not be sold above it. If the purchase price is at or above MRP the store loses money on every unit, so the order is blocked.

## GST invoices
Every supplier must have a valid 15 character GSTIN. The invoice must show the GSTIN, HSN code and tax rate. Orders to a supplier without a GSTIN on file are not placed.

## Price rises
If a supplier raises price by more than 10 percent over the last order, the planner must check market news and record the reason.
