Shree Ganesh Dairy pauses production for five days

11 October 2026

Shree Ganesh Dairy has paused production at its Ghaziabad plant for five days, from 11 to 15 October, after a routine food safety inspection asked for repairs to its chilling unit.

The company said supplies of paneer and milk to retailers will be affected during this period.

Retailers are advised to arrange other suppliers for paneer.
