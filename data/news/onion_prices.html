Onion prices jump 35 percent after rain damage in Nashik

11 October 2026

Wholesale onion prices at Azadpur mandi rose about 35 percent this week after heavy rain damaged stored crop in Nashik and Lasalgaon.

Traders expect prices to stay high for two to three weeks until the new kharif crop arrives.

Retailers report that households are buying larger packs to stock up before prices rise further, pushing up demand for onion.
