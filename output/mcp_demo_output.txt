Tools shared by the RestockPilot MCP server:
  - get_stock_position: Stock on hand, stock on order, usual supplier, shelf space and shelf life for one SKU.
  - get_sales_summary: 28-day sales summary for one SKU (averages, spread, weekend effect, trend).
  - get_upcoming_events: Calendar events (festivals, matches, weather) that affect this SKU in the next `days` days.
  - rank_suppliers: Suppliers for this SKU's category with status and score (40% price, 30% fill, 20% on-time, 10% lead time).
  - scrape_news: Read a market news page and return clean text, with hidden or suspicious text removed.
  - search_policy: Search the procurement policy and supplier knowledge bases.
